## 2. TOUCHPOINT VISUAL AUDIT

### A. Logo Mark & Typography (`imgi_2.jpg`)
* **Visual Geometry:** Monochromatic white line-art graphics enclosed within a circular badge frame.
* **Graphic Symbol:** An iconographic burger featuring a house chimney emitting two curls of smoke from the top bun—a direct visual metaphor combining "House" and "Burger".
* **Typography Style:** Bold, heavy, stencil-influenced display typeface with pronounced slab-serif/block terminals.
* **Color Base:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) line work on Solid Dark/Black (`#000000`) background.

### B. Interior Space & Physical Architecture (`imgi_12`, `imgi_38`, `imgi_31`)
* **Materials & Architectural Palette:**
  * **Exposed Brick / Terracotta:** Rustic brick fireplace/oven (*lareira/churrasqueira de tijolo à vista*).
  * **Dark Wooden Furniture:** Dark stained folding tables and chairs.
  * **Ceiling & Beams:** Exposed ceiling beams painted soft cream/off-white.
  * **Lighting:** Warm Edison filament pendant bulbs emitting amber glow (`2200K-2700K`).
  * **Wall Decor:** Hand-painted mountain landscape mural, glass brick partition, dark matte walls.
  * **Foliage Feature:** Artificial green leaf wall framing a black chalkboard sign ("CARPE DIEM").
  * **Night Atmosphere:** Purple ambient LED ceiling glow for evening lounge vibe.

### C. Packaging & Collateral (`imgi_17`, `imgi_18`, `imgi_21`, `imgi_23`)
* **Branded Wrapping Paper:** Cream/off-white parchment paper printed with charcoal line-art icons (burgers, fries, sodas, hearts, bacon, chef hats) and the circular "HOUSE BURGUER DIADEMA" badge.
* **Staff Attire:** Professional black chef shirt/apron and black baseball cap.
* **Serving Presentation:** White ceramic plates, wooden table surfaces, small tubs of signature green mayonnaise (*maionese verde*).

### D. Photography & Gastronomic Aesthetics (`imgi_17`, `imgi_18`, `imgi_22`, `imgi_34`)
* **Color Saturation:** Rich, warm food tones (golden Brioche buns, toasted Australian dark bread, vibrant yellow melted cheddar, crispy deep-green kale, dark seared beef patties).
* **Lighting Style:** Warm, high-contrast, tactile low-key macro photography focusing on food texture, cheese melt dynamics, and human craft.

### B. Typography Personality Profile
* **Display / Headings:** Grounded, heavy, rustic-artisan personality. Requires a bold, high-impact display font with strong structure, echoing the heavy slab/stencil character of the logo mark.
* **Body / UI Text:** Warm neutral sans-serif with high legibility and soft geometry to balance the heavy display tone.

### C. Graphic Motifs, Shapes & Textures
* **Shapes:** Circles, badges, rounded pill shapes, rectangular wooden frames.
* **Graphic Motifs:** House chimney smoke symbol, hand-drawn ingredient line-art icons (burger, bacon, fries, soda, heart).
* **Textures:** Rustic brickwork, dark wood grain, matte plaster, cream parchment paper, lush foliage.
* **Photography Style:** Authentic, warm-toned, tactile, un-staged food and human photography.
